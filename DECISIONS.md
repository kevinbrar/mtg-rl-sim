- 2026-11-24 (D): A targeted reflexive ("when you do") trigger is queued with the abilities that triggered during the same resolution and placed by the common CR 603.3b path, instead of being pushed during the resolution. Reason: its controller must be able to order it against those abilities (Inti, Seneschal of the Sun; XMage asks). Done in the shared path, not per card. Consequence: a sole legal target is auto-chosen like for any other trigger. Phase patch 0044.
- 2026-11-24 (D): A refused choice inside a prompt is scripted as `expect = "refused"` on the choice step (`choose_copies`, `trigger_targets`): the last entry must be refused after the earlier ones are accepted, and the earlier ones are the real choice. Reason: one step states both the legal choice and the illegal one, so the scenario still ends in a comparable state. Engines differ in how they refuse (Phase rejects a submitted copy selection; Forge stops offering), so only the fact of refusal and the end state are compared. Forge-oracle suites only until a card needs it in XMage. The refused entry must be legal on its own in both engines (fresh-context review of PR #54 showed that a never-legal entry, such as a graveyard card as a target, otherwise counted as refused), so a refused step needs at least one accepted entry, no `@skip`, and no repeated entry.
- 2026-11-25 (D): A scry is scripted with `choose_scry` (labels put on the bottom; the rest stay on top in order), and no runner answers a scry without it. In Phase, a resolution step ends only at a priority window or a finished game. Reason: an unanswered scry used to end the step silently in Phase and keep everything on top in Forge, which made a missing script look like an engine divergence (Opt as an Uldaros copy).
- 2026-11-25 (D): Engine invariants are checked by a seeded random-game fuzzer (`invariant-fuzz`) that needs no oracle: zone integrity, offered actions apply, progress, no panic, bounded length. CI runs 180 games per engine-path change; a violation fails unless its signature is listed in `ci/fuzz-known.txt` with an open `STATUS.md` entry. Reason: scenario suites only cover scripted lines of 18 cards, and the first sweep found a crash, an endless game and inexact action offers that no scenario reached. Known signatures are for offered-then-rejected actions only; crashes, hangs and zone faults are never listed.
- 2026-11-25 (D): The player-loss safety net waits while a spell or ability is resolving or a replacement effect's instructions are unfinished (Phase patch 0045), as it already waits for casts, activations and mana abilities. Reason: state-based actions are checked only when a player would receive priority (CR 704.3) and ignore what happens during a resolution (CR 704.4). Cost: a scenario ending in a loss does not compare the loser's board, so MP3 does not get XMage confirmation of the land's final state. The reach check `lost` asserts the loser.
